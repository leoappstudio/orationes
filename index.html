About the translations

Traditional translations are used where available. These texts are intended for prayer, study and reference and are not presented as the current official liturgical translations of any bishops’ conference.

The Latin texts are traditional forms. Some translations therefore use older established wording rather than modern liturgical translations.

Privacy

Orationes – Prayers does not require an account and is not intended to collect, store or share personal information. The app contains no advertising or user tracking.
